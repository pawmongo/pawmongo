# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

phpMongo welcomes contributors and users of every background and experience level. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to help maintain a respectful environment, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and code.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experience levels, and personal boundaries.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private information and obtain consent before sharing it.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized comments or unwanted advances, personal attacks, sustained disruption, and publishing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in its resolution is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project community spaces. It also applies when someone represents phpMongo in public.

## Reporting a concern

Contact a project maintainer privately through a contact method listed on their repository profile. If that maintainer is involved in the concern, contact another maintainer when one is available. Share what happened, where it happened, and relevant evidence. Do not publish private information in a public issue.

A dedicated community reporting address has not yet been designated. Maintainers should publish one before opening additional community channels. This policy does not promise a private reporting feature or contact method that the repository has not configured.

## Maintainer responsibilities and enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly, limit disclosure to people needed to resolve the concern, and explain decisions to the people involved where appropriate. They may remove inappropriate content and ask participants to stop harmful behavior.

Responses depend on severity, impact, and repetition: a correction or warning, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban. Serious threats or harassment may warrant immediate removal. Anyone contesting an enforcement decision may request a review privately from an uninvolved maintainer when one is available.

Maintainers are subject to the same expectations as other participants.
